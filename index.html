Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is SUSKII?

SUSKII is a technology company and the parent brand of SUSKII Marketplace, SUSKII Errands and SUSKII Travel & Tours. Each is an independent platform within the SUSKII ecosystem.

Which SUSKII platforms can I use today?

SUSKII Marketplace is live on the web at suskii.com and on Android via Google Play. SUSKII Errands is coming soon, and SUSKII Travel & Tours is in development.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. SUSKII Marketplace for iOS is coming soon. Until then, iPhone users can use the Marketplace on the web at suskii.com.

Where is SUSKII available?

SUSKII Marketplace is currently available in Nigeria and Rwanda, with plans to expand to more countries.

Is SUSKII free to use?

Creating an account and browsing products on SUSKII Marketplace is free.

Can I book travel with SUSKII yet?

No. SUSKII Travel & Tours is in development and bookings are not yet available. Register your interest and we’ll let you know when it launches.
